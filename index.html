Inflation

Inflation means the prices of goods and services increase over time. This can reduce what the same amount of money can buy.

EXAMPLE

A product costing ₹100 today may cost ₹110 later if prices increase by 10%.

OUR PRINCIPLE

Information should be understandable.

Financial information can be difficult to understand, especially when it comes through social media, messaging apps, or unfamiliar terminology.

FinVoice focuses on helping users understand information and recognize warning signs — without making investment decisions for them.

✓ Education-first ✓ Privacy-focused ✓ Clear explanations ✓ No investment recommendations
SAFETY & PRIVACY

Your financial safety comes first.

FinVoice is designed to help you understand financial information and identify possible warning signs before you take action.

🛡

Educational, not financial advice

FinVoice does not recommend specific investments, predict market outcomes, or guarantee financial returns.

🔒

Protect sensitive information

Never share your OTP, PIN, password, CVV, or other sensitive financial information through messages or untrusted links.

✓

Verify before you act

Always verify financial claims through trusted, official sources before making payments or investments.

Important: FinVoice is an educational and safety-support tool. It does not provide investment advice or guarantee financial outcomes. Always verify information independently before taking financial action.
